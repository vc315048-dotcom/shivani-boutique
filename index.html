Policies & FAQs

Shipping & Delivery

Delivery availability, charges and estimated timelines depend on the item and delivery location. Please confirm these details with the boutique on WhatsApp before placing your order.

Returns & Exchanges

Return or exchange eligibility may depend on the product and whether it is custom-stitched. Please ask for the applicable policy in writing before confirming an order. Do not assume a return is accepted unless the boutique confirms it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I confirm the price and availability?

Choose the item and message the boutique on WhatsApp. Confirm the final price, fabric, size, availability and delivery details before ordering.

Can I request custom stitching?

Yes, use the Custom Stitching & Style Form on this page. It opens WhatsApp with your preferences so the boutique can discuss measurements and design options with you.

Can I pay directly on the website?

This version does not collect online payments. Orders are discussed with the boutique on WhatsApp; confirm the official payment method directly with them before paying.

Can I return a custom-stitched item?

Custom orders may have different conditions. Please get the applicable return or alteration terms confirmed by the boutique before placing the order.

Are the displayed prices final?

Prices shown are indicative product listings. Confirm the final price and availability with the boutique before ordering.
